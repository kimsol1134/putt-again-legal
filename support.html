Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Core play and local saving in the current version work offline.

Do I need an account?
No. You can play immediately without signing in.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. The current version is a paid complete edition with neither advertising nor in-app purchases.

Where is progress stored?
Only in the app's local iOS container. Deleting the app may also delete progress.

Why is there no sound?
In the app's Settings, confirm that Game Sound and the relevant Sound Effects, Ambience, and Music switches are on. PUTT AGAIN respects the iPhone silent switch and mixes with audio from other apps, so also check the device volume and silent mode.

What to include in a support request

Include your device model, iOS version, app version and build number, the screen where the issue occurred, reproduction steps, and—if possible—a screenshot without personal information. Do not send passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other sensitive data.
