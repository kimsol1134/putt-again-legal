Privacy Policy

전생빨로 프로 골퍼 / PUTT AGAIN · 2026-09-05

Game progress stays on your device. Limited usage summaries are sent to Firebase Analytics only while optional analytics is enabled.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to the iPhone game PUTT AGAIN. The operator is Sol Kim. For privacy questions, contact kimsol1134@gmail.com or +82 10-2966-9554.

2. Optional usage analytics

No account is required. Starting with version 1.3, usage analytics is off by default for new installations. If you enable anonymous usage-data sharing in Settings, limited usage information is sent to Google Firebase Analytics to help improve the app. A previously saved choice is preserved when you update.

3. Information included in analytics

When enabled, analytics processes a randomly generated app-installation identifier, device and operating-system information, app version and language, approximate region derived from an IP address, app launch and session information, and bounded events such as life or season number, distance range, progress, and success or failure. The App Store categories are Device ID, Coarse Location, and Product Interaction.

4. Exclusions and tracking

We do not send account names, email addresses, contacts, photos, precise location, advertising identifiers, raw putting inputs, ball coordinates or trajectories, or complete game-save files through analytics. Google Signals and User-ID are not used. Ad personalization and cross-app tracking are disabled. We do not associate analytics with your real-world identity or sell it to data brokers.

5. Game data stored on your device

Your golfer, career, past lives, memories, and preferences for sound, haptics, sensitivity, and analytics are stored on the device. The core game works offline. Enabling analytics does not upload your complete save file.

6. Service and retention

Optional analytics is processed by Google Firebase Analytics, with encrypted transmission. Event- and user-level data retention is configured to 14 months. Processing on Google infrastructure may take place outside your country. We use analytics to understand the play flow and problem areas and improve the game.

7. Changing your choice and deletion requests

Turning usage-data sharing off in Settings stops subsequent analytics collection and resets the installation analytics identifier. This does not immediately delete every event previously sent to the service. Contact us with privacy questions or analytics deletion requests. Because the app has no account system, we may be unable to identify the analytics records associated with a particular request. You can delete local game progress through the data-reset control in Settings.

8. Changes

When data practices change, we update this policy, the in-app notice, and the App Store privacy disclosure. The effective date of this policy is September 5, 2026.
